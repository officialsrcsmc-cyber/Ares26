Our Mission & Goal

Fostering a culture of research, innovation, and academic excellence. We equip future healthcare professionals with the skills, ethics, and confidence to lead impactful research in medicine and public health.

What we do

Three ways SRC helps students grow as researchers.

Workshops

Hands-on sessions led by invited faculty on research methodology, scientific writing and data analysis.

Mentorship

Pairing students with experienced researchers to shape ideas into projects, abstracts and papers.

Research projects

Guided support from idea to data collection — turning pilot studies and student-led projects into results.
