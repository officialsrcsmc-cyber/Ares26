Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Draft pending committee review. This policy was written from how the website and the ARES’26 registration app actually work in the source code. Items highlighted in yellow need the SRC committee to confirm before this notice is treated as final. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

1. Who we are

This website (srcsmc.org) and the ARES’26 registration app (srcsmc.org/ares26) are run by SRC at Sargodha Medical College, Sargodha, Pakistan. Contact: official.srcsmc@gmail.com. 2. What we collect

Browsing the SRC homepage – no account is needed and the page does not ask you for personal information. Our hosting provider records standard technical logs (such as IP address and requested page) to deliver and secure the site.

Creating an ARES’26 account – first and last name, email address, password, whether you are an SMC student, phone number, institution, programme, degree year, and the time you accepted the Terms. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider; the site itself never stores or sees them in readable form.

Payment details – transaction date and time, account name, amount, transaction ID, and the payment-proof image or PDF you upload. We do not take card payments on this site.

Abstract submissions – title, discipline, category, the Introduction / Methodology / Results / Conclusion text, and any supplementary file you attach (PDF, DOCX or image).

Status and administration – your registration status (Pending, Approved, Rejected) and abstract review status, set by the organising team.

3. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and confirm your email address.
  • To verify payments and manage your ARES’26 registration.
  • To receive, review and respond to abstracts.
  • To email you about your account and registration status (confirmation, password reset and, if enabled, payment-received / approved / rejected notices).
  • To keep the site secure and, only if you allow it, to understand how it is used (see section 6).

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

4. Who can see it

Your profile, payment details and abstracts are visible to you and to the ARES’26 organising team’s administrator accounts. Uploaded payment proofs and abstract files are stored in private storage; they are opened only through short-lived (five-minute) signed links by you or an administrator. [Owner to confirm: how many administrators have access and whether they are all organising-committee members]

5. Service providers we rely on

  • Supabase – authentication, database and file storage for ARES’26. [Owner to confirm: Supabase project region / where the data is hosted]
  • Vercel – website hosting, and (if enabled by us and you consent) cookieless Web Analytics.
  • Cloudflare – domain name (DNS) services. [Owner to confirm: whether Cloudflare proxying/CDN is turned on for the site]
  • Google Fonts – the site loads its typefaces from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details when the fonts are requested.
  • Email delivery – account emails are sent through an SMTP provider configured in Supabase[Owner to confirm: which provider, e.g. Resend, Brevo, Mailgun or Gmail]. If the optional status-email function is deployed, Resend sends those messages.
  • WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn – links from our homepage open those services; their own privacy policies apply once you leave our site.

These providers may process data outside Pakistan.

6. Cookies, local storage and analytics

Essential (always on). When you log in to ARES’26, our authentication library keeps your session in your browser’s local storage so you stay signed in. We also store your cookie choice in local storage (key src_consent_v1). These are strictly necessary and are not used to track you.

Analytics (off until you opt in). If you choose “Accept analytics”, we load Vercel Web Analytics, which records page views and clicks on key buttons (for example “Register for ARES’26”) and successful form submissions without using cookies or storing personal identifiers. We never send passwords, form contents, uploads or other personal details to analytics. If you choose “Reject non-essential”, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, no analytics script is loaded. You can change your choice any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer.

8. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Database access is restricted so participants can read only their own records, payment proofs and abstract files are stored privately, and participants cannot change their own approval status. No online system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to withdraw consent for analytics (use “Cookie settings”). Email official.srcsmc@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will respond. [Owner to confirm: target response time, and which data-protection law applies.
